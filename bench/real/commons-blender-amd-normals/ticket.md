# File:Blender 2.9 AMD normals bug.webm

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blender_2.9_AMD_normals_bug.webm  (reporter: YuriNikolai, filed 2021-01-11)

A screen recording of a bug introduced around the time of Blender 2.9's release by an update to some AMD drivers. Many people had reported this bug, and it ended up being [fixed](https://developer.blender.org/T82856) in the Blender 2.91.2. release (well ahead of schedule!), so I'm glad i got a screen capture of it for posterity without the program crashing. If you used Eevee, this had to do with not using High Quality Normals. Some people got this error on Cycles too, with no solution except reverting to older AMD drivers. I believe AMD Pro line drivers were free of this glitch, but couldn't test it.
The texture shown in the video is from PolyHaven and licensed as CC0. Recorded with OBS, converted with Shotcut.
